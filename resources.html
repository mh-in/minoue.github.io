Resources

Tools and reference material based on our papers, made for people who observe and collect myxomycetes — an attempt to connect research with naturalists. Most of these are currently in Japanese.

Tools

  • Identification aid for Reticularia (prototype)

    Enter the characters you can observe to narrow down the candidate species; the tool suggests the next character to check and shows reference images from the paper. A Sanger read can also be matched against the reference sequences to find the closest species and ribotype.

    Based on Leontyev et al. (2026) Fungal Systematics and Evolution 17: 19–29 and its supplementary material. A prototype built on a single paper — an aid to identification, not a final determination.

    Japanese, with an English summary · published by an author of the paper

Reference material

  • English–Japanese glossary of myxomycete morphology

    Morphological terms used in English literature, with the standard Japanese terms and page references in Yamamoto (2021) Biota of Japanese Myxomycetes.

    Japanese and English · updated as terms are checked

  • Guide for specimen contributors

    Guidelines for naturalists who would like to contribute myxomycete specimens: collecting, storing and sending specimens, what to record, and how specimens and personal information are handled.

    Japanese
